Wedding Feast

The nalangu is done, now it's time for the feast! You're the server (panjaayat waiter) at a South Indian wedding. Guests will shout for food — run to the kitchen counter, grab the right dish, and deliver it before they turn hangry.

How to play

  1. 🍽Watch the tables. A food icon floats above any table that has an order. The bar below it shows time remaining.
  2. 🍛Go to the counter (the top row). Walk to the column with the dish you need — you'll pick it up automatically.
  3. 🏃Rush to the table. Get close and the dish is delivered automatically if it's the right one.
  4. 😤Don't be late! Expired orders make guests angry — they fight their relatives and the wedding happiness drops.

Three rounds

  • BrBreakfast — Idli, Dosa, Pongal, Vada, Coffee. 4 tables, generous time.
  • LuLunch — Rice, Sambar, Rasam, Kootu, Payasam. 6 tables, medium pace.
  • DiDinner — Biryani, Parotta, Kurma, Kothu, Jigarthanda. 9 tables, fast orders.

Arrow keys or WASD to move. P to pause, R to restart.Swipe on the hall or use the pad below.
