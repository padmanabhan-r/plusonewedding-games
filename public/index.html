PlusOne Wedding

A pixel maze game about the week before an Indian wedding. You're the couple-to-be: collect every RSVP, then run up the aisle to the mandap before the chaos catches you.

How to play

  1. Collect every RSVP in the hall.
  2. Dodge the chaos. Get caught and you lose a day. You have three.
  3. Grab the power-ups as they appear. They're listed below.
  4. Run up the aisle. With every RSVP in, the mandap opens. Reach it to clear the level.

Power-ups

  • The +1 isn't a life. For a few seconds the chaos panics, and touching them seats them for points.
  • Extra day shows up once a level if you've lost a day. Grab it to win that day back.

The chaos

  • Aunty Inquiry stops you for a chat. Mash the arrows to escape.
  • Gatecrasher walks in uninvited, and keeps multiplying.
  • Budget Blowout floats over tables, faster every level.
  • The DJ plays requests you didn't make. One hit flips your controls.

Arrow keys or WASD to move, P to pause, R to restart.Swipe on the hall or use the pad to move. Five levels so far. New levels coming soon.
